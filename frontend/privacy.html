Privacy Policy

Depending on how you use Orymart, we may collect information such as:

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law or lawful authority.

8. Account Security

You are responsible for keeping your account credentials and device secure.

If you believe that your account has been accessed without authorization, contact Orymart support as soon as possible.

9. Children's Privacy

Orymart is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate parental or legal consent.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including requesting access, correction or deletion of certain information.
