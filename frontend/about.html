Who We Are

Orymart is a local online grocery store serving customers in Afzalpur, Karnataka. We provide everyday grocery and essential products, including dairy products, snacks, beverages, household essentials, cleaning products and personal care items.

Orymart is being built to make everyday grocery shopping simple and convenient for our local community.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make everyday grocery shopping more convenient by bringing essential products closer to customers through a simple online shopping experience.
